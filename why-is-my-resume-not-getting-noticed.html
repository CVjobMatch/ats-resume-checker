What’s actually happening

Before a recruiter sees your resume, it goes through an ATS.

Resume → ATS Scan → Match Score → Seen (or ignored)

If your match score is too low, your resume is never noticed.

Main reasons your resume is ignored

  • Your resume doesn’t match the job description
  • You’re missing important keywords
  • Your content is too generic
  • Your experience is not clearly aligned

Most resumes fail because they don’t match closely enough.

Quick check

  • Does your resume include the exact job title?
  • Are your skills written exactly like the job post?
  • Does your experience clearly match the role?

If not, your resume is being ignored.

Example

Job: "Customer Support"
Resume: "Worked with customers"

This weak match reduces your chances of being noticed.

How to fix it

  • Match your resume to the job
  • Use correct keywords
  • Improve skills
  • Fix your summary
