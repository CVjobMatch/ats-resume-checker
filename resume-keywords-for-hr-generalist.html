HR Generalist resumes should include human resources, HR operations, recruitment, talent acquisition, onboarding, employee relations, performance management, payroll, benefits administration, HR policies, HR compliance, labor law, employment law, HRIS, Workday, ADP, BambooHR, SAP SuccessFactors, employee engagement, training and development, conflict resolution, and HR analytics when they match your experience.

How do I make my HR Generalist resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact HR Generalist keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on HR operations, employee relations, recruitment, onboarding, HRIS systems, payroll, benefits, compliance, policies, and measurable HR outcomes.

Why is my HR Generalist resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked with staff” do not show whether you managed employee relations, handled onboarding, used Workday or ADP, supported payroll, ensured HR compliance, coached managers, or improved retention.

Should I include HR systems and measurable results on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. HR Generalist roles often search for HRIS, Workday, ADP, BambooHR, SAP SuccessFactors, Greenhouse, Lever, ATS systems, HR analytics, reduced turnover, faster time-to-hire, onboarding completion, employee engagement, and compliance readiness.
