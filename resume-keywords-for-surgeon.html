Surgeon resume FAQ

What keywords should a Surgeon put on a CV?

Surgeon CVs should include surgical procedures, operating room, operating theatre, laparoscopic surgery, open surgery, trauma surgery, sterile technique, suturing, surgical instrumentation, patient safety, preoperative assessment, postoperative care, ICU coordination, clinical documentation, and surgical team leadership when they match your experience.

How do I make my Surgeon CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact surgical keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on procedures, specialty, operating room experience, preoperative and postoperative care, patient safety, documentation, leadership, and clinical outcomes.

Why is my Surgeon CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in hospital” do not show whether you performed specific procedures, managed operating room workflows, handled preoperative or postoperative care, followed sterile protocols, or improved patient outcomes.

Should I include surgical specialties and procedures on my CV?

Yes. Surgeon roles often search for specialty and procedure keywords such as general surgery, laparoscopic surgery, open surgery, trauma surgery, orthopedic surgery, cardiovascular surgery, neurosurgery, vascular surgery, emergency surgery, and elective procedures when they match your experience.
