What ATS and recruiters may look for

  • SQL and data querying experience
  • Excel, Power BI, Tableau, or other reporting tools
  • Data cleaning, validation, and modeling skills
  • Dashboard development and KPI reporting
  • Business insights, stakeholder communication, and decision support
  • Projects or real-world data work that prove the skills

Example of a weak Data Analyst resume match

Job description: "SQL, dashboard development, KPI reporting, business insights"
Resume says: "Worked with data and helped reports"

Worked with data • Helped reports • Assisted team

This wording is too vague. It does not clearly show tools, responsibilities, or business value.

Better Data Analyst resume wording

Analyzed datasets using SQL, built KPI dashboards in Power BI, cleaned and transformed data, and delivered insights that supported business decisions.

The stronger version gives clearer Data Analyst keywords and shows a better match with the job description.

What many Data Analyst resumes miss

  • No SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI, or Tableau mentioned clearly
  • No dashboard, reporting, or KPI language
  • No measurable business impact
  • No project, portfolio, or real-world data examples
  • Too much generic language and not enough data-specific wording
