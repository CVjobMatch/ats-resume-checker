Step-by-step: How to write a resume that works

1. Start with a relevant summary

Focus on skills and experience that match the job you’re applying for.

2. Show results, not tasks

Example: “Increased sales by 20%” instead of “Responsible for sales.”

3. Use exact keywords

Take keywords directly from the job description.

4. Keep formatting simple

Avoid complex layouts. ATS systems prefer clean formatting.
