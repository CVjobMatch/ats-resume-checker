Technical Superintendent resume FAQ

What keywords should a Technical Superintendent put on a CV?

Technical Superintendent CVs should include fleet management, vessel operations, PMS, planned maintenance, dry dock, vessel inspections, ISM Code, SMS, class compliance, flag state, technical audits, ship management, maintenance planning, budget control, marine engineering, and regulatory compliance when they match your experience.

How do I make my Technical Superintendent CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact maritime technical keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on fleet management, vessel inspections, maintenance planning, dry dock, PMS, compliance, class requirements, budget control, vessel types, and ship management responsibilities.

Why is my Technical Superintendent CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked as engineer” do not show whether you managed vessels, planned maintenance, coordinated dry dock, handled inspections, controlled budgets, ensured class compliance, or supported fleet operations.

Should I include vessel types on my Technical Superintendent CV?

Yes. Include vessel types such as tankers, offshore vessels, bulk carriers, container ships, LNG carriers, chemical tankers, product tankers, supply vessels, cargo vessels, or cruise ships when they match your experience.
