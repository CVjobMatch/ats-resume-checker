Skills section template

A simple skills section should combine hard skills, tools, and role-specific keywords from the job description. 

Skills
- [Skill from the job description]
- [Tool, software, or system]
- [Relevant method or process]
- [Industry keyword]
- [Certification or requirement]

Why many skills sections fail

Communication • Teamwork • Hardworking • Motivated • Fast learner

These words are not always bad, but they are usually too generic on their own. They become stronger when supported by specific tools, responsibilities, results, or job-related keywords.

How to choose the right skills

  • Match your skills to the job description
  • Use relevant resume keywords
  • Start with the actual job post, not a generic skills list
  • Prioritize hard skills, tools, systems, and certifications
  • Remove weak skills that do not support the role
  • Make sure your experience proves the skills you list

Example: weak vs stronger skills wording

Job description: "CRM, customer follow-up, ticket resolution"
Weak resume skills: "Communication, teamwork, admin"

CRM • Customer follow-up • Ticket resolution • Complaint handling • Customer support

The stronger version is closer to the job description and gives clearer signals about your fit.
