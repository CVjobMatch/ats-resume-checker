Yacht Steward resume FAQ

What keywords should a Yacht Steward or Stewardess put on a CV?

Yacht Steward and Stewardess CVs should include superyacht service, luxury hospitality, guest service, cabin service, housekeeping, laundry service, table service, silver service, interior maintenance, guest experience, discretion, attention to detail, provisioning, STCW basic safety, and ENG1 when they match your experience.

How do I make my Yacht Steward CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the yacht job description and add the exact yacht keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on luxury guest service, housekeeping, cabin preparation, laundry, silver service, table service, interior maintenance, STCW, ENG1, and yacht or high-end hospitality experience.

Why is my Yacht Steward CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in hospitality” do not show whether you provided superyacht service, prepared cabins, handled laundry and linen care, delivered silver service, tracked guest preferences, or worked with STCW safety standards.

Should I include STCW and ENG1 on my Yacht Steward CV?

Yes, if you have them. Yacht recruiters often look for STCW basic safety and ENG1 medical certification, especially for onboard yacht and superyacht roles.
