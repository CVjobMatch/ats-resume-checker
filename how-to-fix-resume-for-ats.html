What ATS looks for

Resume → Keyword Match → Score → Recruiter

If your score is too low, your resume gets filtered out automatically.

Step-by-step: fix your resume

Step 1: Match your resume to the job

Use exact wording from the job description.

Step 2: Add missing keywords

Include relevant tools, skills, and job titles.

👉 Use a keyword optimizer

Step 3: Simplify formatting

Avoid tables, columns, and graphics.

Step 4: Focus on relevance

Remove experience that doesn’t match the job.

Step 5: Improve clarity

Use clear, measurable achievements.
