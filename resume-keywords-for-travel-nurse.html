Travel Nurse resume FAQ

What keywords should a Travel Nurse put on a resume?

Travel Nurse resumes should include travel nursing, contract nursing, patient care, rapid onboarding, adaptability, multi-site experience, EHR, clinical documentation, medication administration, patient safety, care coordination, acute care, telemetry, ICU, ER, and interdisciplinary collaboration when they match your experience.

How do I make my Travel Nurse resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact travel nursing keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on clinical specialties, licenses, certifications, EHR systems, patient care duties, facility types, rapid onboarding, and documentation.

Why is my Travel Nurse resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked as nurse” do not show whether you handled travel assignments, adapted to new units, used EHR systems, administered medications, worked in specific specialties, or maintained patient safety.

Should I include certifications and state licenses on my Travel Nurse resume?

Yes, if they match the job requirements. Travel nurse recruiters often search for RN license, compact license, state nursing licenses, BLS, ACLS, PALS, TNCC, NIHSS, CCRN, and other specialty certifications.
