Why your resume doesn’t match

  • Missing keywords from the job posting
  • Using different wording
  • Including irrelevant experience
  • Sending the same resume everywhere

Most resumes only match 30–55% of job requirements.

Example

Job description: "Customer Support"
Resume: "Worked with clients"

This is not considered a match by ATS systems.

Quick check

  • Does your resume include the exact job title?
  • Are your skills written exactly like the job description?
  • Does your experience clearly match the role?

If not, your match score is too low.

How to fix it

  • Fix missing keywords
  • Use the right keywords
  • Improve your skills
  • Fix your summary
