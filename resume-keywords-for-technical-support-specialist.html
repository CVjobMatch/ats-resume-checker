Technical Support Specialist resume FAQ

What keywords should a Technical Support Specialist put on a resume?

Technical Support Specialist resumes should include troubleshooting, help desk, IT support, ticketing systems, Zendesk, ServiceNow, Jira, customer support, hardware support, software support, Windows, MacOS, networking, Active Directory, remote support, system diagnostics, and problem resolution when they match your experience.

How do I make my Technical Support Specialist resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact technical support keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on troubleshooting, ticketing systems, operating systems, networking, remote support, customer communication, escalation handling, and measurable support outcomes.

Why is my Technical Support Specialist resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “helped users” do not show whether you handled help desk tickets, used Zendesk or ServiceNow, supported Windows or MacOS, managed Active Directory requests, troubleshot networks, or met SLA targets.

Should I include ticketing tools and operating systems on my resume?

Yes, if you have used them. Technical Support Specialist roles often search for tools and systems such as Zendesk, ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Freshdesk, Windows, MacOS, Linux, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Active Directory, VPN, Okta, and remote support tools.
