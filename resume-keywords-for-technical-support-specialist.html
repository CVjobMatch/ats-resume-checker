Top technical support specialist resume keywords

What ATS looks for in technical support resumes

  • Experience with troubleshooting and problem solving
  • Knowledge of systems (Windows, MacOS, networking)
  • Ticketing tools (Zendesk, Jira, ServiceNow)
  • Customer support and communication skills
  • Technical issue resolution experience
