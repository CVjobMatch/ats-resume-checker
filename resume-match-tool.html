How resume matching actually works

Resume → Keyword Match → Relevance Score → Interview or Rejection

ATS systems and recruiters look for signs that your resume matches the job description. That includes keywords, skills, tools, responsibilities, seniority, and role-specific wording.

If the wording is too generic or too far from the job description, your application can look weaker than it really is.

Example of why resumes fail

Job requires: "Project management, stakeholder communication, delivery tracking"
Your CV says: "Managed tasks and helped projects"

Managed tasks • Helped projects • Assisted team

This may be true, but it does not create a strong resume match.

Better example

Led project management activities • Coordinated stakeholders • Tracked delivery progress and deadlines

The improved version uses clearer job-specific wording and makes the connection to the role easier to understand.
