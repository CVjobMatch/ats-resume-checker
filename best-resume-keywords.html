What are resume keywords?

Resume keywords are the words and phrases employers use in job descriptions to describe the role. They can include job titles, skills, tools, responsibilities, certifications, systems, methods, and industry terms.

  • Job titles and role variations
  • Hard skills and software tools
  • Industry-specific terms
  • Certifications and licenses
  • Processes, responsibilities, and methods
  • Soft skills when they are important for the role

Why resume keywords matter

Resume → Job Description → Keyword Match → Match Score → Interview or Rejection

ATS systems and recruiters often scan for signs that your resume matches the job description. If your resume uses generic wording or misses important terms, your application can look less relevant than it really is.

Why resumes fail keyword checks

  • The resume does not include important keywords from the job description
  • The wording is too generic
  • The same resume is sent to every job
  • Relevant tools, systems, or certifications are missing
  • Strong experience is described with weak or vague wording

Quick keyword check

  • Does your resume include the exact job title or a close variation?
  • Do your skills match the wording in the job description?
  • Are relevant tools, systems, certifications, and methods included?
  • Are the strongest keywords visible near the top of the resume?
  • Does your experience support the keywords you list?

How to choose the right resume keywords

  • Match your resume to the job description
  • Use important wording from the job post naturally
  • Focus on relevant keywords, not every word in the posting
  • Rewrite vague bullet points into role-specific achievements
  • Check whether your resume match score improves before applying
