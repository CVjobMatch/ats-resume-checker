Systems and documentation keywords

SMS • PMS • PTW • Maintenance records • Safety reports • Voyage planning • Compliance documentation • Crew coordination

Why maritime CVs get rejected or ignored

  • Important maritime keywords are missing
  • The CV does not clearly match the job description
  • Vessel types, certifications, or systems are not visible
  • Compliance terms such as ISM, SOLAS, MARPOL, STCW, or ISPS are missing when relevant
  • Experience is described too generally without operational detail
  • Results such as safety, uptime, cost control, inspections, or maintenance performance are not shown

Weak vs stronger maritime CV wording

Job description: "PMS, preventive maintenance, engine room operations, safety compliance"

Worked in engine room and handled maintenance tasks.
Performed engine room operations, updated PMS records, completed preventive maintenance, and supported safety compliance under vessel procedures.

The stronger version gives clearer maritime keywords, systems, responsibilities, and compliance signals.

How ATS and recruiters evaluate maritime CVs

  • Job titles such as Chief Engineer, Deck Cadet, Marine Superintendent, or Able Seaman
  • Certifications, licenses, and compliance terms such as STCW, ISM, SOLAS, and MARPOL
  • Vessel types such as offshore, tanker, yacht, cargo, cruise, or DP vessels
  • Systems such as PMS, SMS, PTW, and planned maintenance tools
  • Relevant experience aligned with the exact job posting
  • Operational impact such as safety, uptime, inspections, audits, or cost control
