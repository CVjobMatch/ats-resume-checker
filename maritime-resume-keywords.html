Why maritime CVs get rejected

  • Missing key maritime keywords
  • Too generic job descriptions
  • No mention of compliance (ISM, SOLAS, MARPOL)
  • Experience not aligned with job requirements

How ATS works for maritime jobs

Most companies scan your CV for exact matches:

  • Job title (Chief Engineer, OIM, etc.)
  • Systems (PMS, SMS, PTW)
  • Certifications and compliance
  • Vessel types (offshore, tanker, yacht)

How to improve your maritime resume or CV

  • Match your CV to each job description
  • Use exact maritime keywords
  • Include vessel types and systems
  • Show measurable impact (safety, uptime, cost)
