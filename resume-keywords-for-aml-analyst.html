What ATS and recruiters may look for

  • AML, KYC, CDD, EDD, and transaction monitoring experience
  • Suspicious activity investigations and SAR or STR reporting
  • Sanctions screening, PEP screening, adverse media checks, and watchlist review
  • Financial crime, fraud detection, regulatory compliance, and risk assessment knowledge
  • Experience with banks, fintech companies, crypto companies, payments, or regulated financial services
  • Familiarity with AML tools, case management systems, screening platforms, or monitoring systems
  • Clear evidence that you can investigate alerts, document findings, escalate risks, and follow compliance procedures

Example of a weak AML Analyst resume match

Job description: "Transaction monitoring, AML investigations, KYC, CDD, EDD, SAR reporting, sanctions screening"
Resume says: "Worked in compliance"

Worked in compliance • Helped reports • Assisted team • Reviewed customer files

This wording is too vague. It does not clearly show AML investigations, transaction monitoring, due diligence, suspicious activity reporting, sanctions screening, or the specific compliance tasks recruiters are looking for.

Better AML Analyst resume wording

Performed AML transaction monitoring, reviewed suspicious activity alerts, conducted KYC, CDD, and EDD checks, documented case findings, escalated high-risk customers, and supported SAR reporting for potential financial crime cases.

The stronger version gives clearer AML Analyst keywords and shows a better match with the job description.

What many AML Analyst resumes miss

  • No clear AML, KYC, CDD, EDD, or transaction monitoring language
  • No SAR, STR, suspicious activity, escalation, or case investigation keywords
  • No sanctions screening, PEP screening, adverse media, or watchlist review detail
  • No mention of regulatory frameworks such as FATF, BSA, FinCEN, OFAC, AMLD, FCA, FINTRAC, MAS, or AUSTRAC when relevant
  • No AML tools, screening platforms, transaction monitoring systems, or case management systems
  • No measurable investigation, alert review, backlog reduction, quality improvement, or risk reduction impact
  • Too much generic compliance language and not enough AML-specific wording

AML Analyst resume FAQ

What keywords should an AML Analyst put on a resume?

AML Analyst resumes should include anti-money laundering, KYC, CDD, EDD, transaction monitoring, SAR reporting, STR reporting, suspicious activity, sanctions screening, PEP screening, adverse media, fraud detection, risk assessment, and compliance investigations.

How do I make my AML Analyst resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact AML keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on specific duties such as alert review, transaction monitoring, KYC checks, due diligence, sanctions screening, case documentation, escalation, and SAR support.

Why is my AML Analyst resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in compliance” do not show whether you handled AML investigations, transaction monitoring, SAR reporting, KYC, sanctions screening, or financial crime risk.

Should I include AML tools on my resume?

Yes, if you have used them. Include relevant tools such as Actimize, World-Check, LexisNexis, Dow Jones Risk & Compliance, Fircosoft, ComplyAdvantage, Chainalysis, Elliptic, Excel, SQL, or case management systems when they match your experience.
