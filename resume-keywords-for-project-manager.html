Top Project Manager resume keywords

What ATS looks for in project manager resumes

  • Experience managing projects end-to-end
  • Stakeholder communication and leadership
  • Knowledge of Agile, Scrum, or Waterfall methodologies
  • Budgeting, scheduling, and delivery tracking
  • Risk management and performance reporting

  • Show measurable impact (delivery time, budget savings, KPIs)
  • Match your resume to each job
