What ATS and recruiters may look for

  • Experience managing projects from planning to delivery
  • Stakeholder communication and cross-functional coordination
  • Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, Jira, Confluence, PMP, or PRINCE2 when relevant
  • Budget tracking, scheduling, delivery tracking, and resource planning
  • Risk management, issue management, change management, and status reporting
  • Measurable outcomes such as delivery time, cost savings, risk reduction, or KPI improvements

Example of a weak Project Manager resume match

Job description: "Project planning, stakeholder management, delivery tracking, Agile"
Resume says: "Managed projects"

Managed projects • Helped teams • Assisted work

This wording is too vague. It does not clearly show project management methods, responsibilities, tools, or measurable delivery outcomes.

Better Project Manager resume wording

Led project planning and delivery tracking, managed stakeholders across cross-functional teams, and used Agile methods to deliver projects on time and within budget.

The stronger version gives clearer Project Manager keywords and shows a better match with the job description.

What many Project Manager resumes miss

  • No clear delivery tracking, timeline management, or milestone language
  • No stakeholder management or cross-functional team detail
  • No Agile, Scrum, Jira, PMP, PRINCE2, or methodology keywords when relevant
  • No budget, risk, resource planning, or status reporting language
  • No measurable impact such as on-time delivery, cost savings, or KPI improvement

How to improve your Project Manager resume

  • Show planning, delivery, stakeholder management, risk, budget, and reporting clearly
  • Include tools and methods such as Jira, Confluence, Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, PMP, or PRINCE2 when relevant
