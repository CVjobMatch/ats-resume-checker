Typical ICU Nurse responsibilities

  • Provide critical care nursing for high-acuity and unstable patients in intensive care settings
  • Manage ventilated patients, monitor respiratory status, assess ABGs, and support airway care
  • Monitor hemodynamics, cardiac status, vital signs, neurological status, and changes in patient condition
  • Administer and titrate IV medications, vasopressors, sedatives, and emergency medications safely
  • Respond to rapid response events, code blue situations, sepsis protocols, and emergency interventions
  • Maintain accurate ICU documentation, nursing notes, handoff reports, and EHR or EMR records
  • Collaborate with intensivists, physicians, nurses, respiratory therapists, pharmacists, and multidisciplinary ICU teams

ICU Nurse resume FAQ

What keywords should an ICU Nurse put on a resume?

ICU Nurse resumes should include ICU Nurse, critical care, intensive care, ventilator management, ventilator support, hemodynamic monitoring, cardiac monitoring, patient stabilization, IV medication administration, vasopressors, sedation management, rapid response, ACLS, BLS, CCRN, Epic, Cerner, clinical documentation, patient safety, and infection control when they match your experience.

How do I make my ICU Nurse resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact ICU Nurse keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on critical care duties, ICU procedures, monitoring skills, certifications, EMR systems, patient safety, documentation, and measurable ICU workload.

Why is my ICU Nurse resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in hospital” do not show whether you managed ventilated patients, monitored hemodynamics, administered IV medications, responded to code blue events, used Epic or Cerner, or handled high-acuity ICU patients.

Should I include certifications and ICU workload on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. ICU Nurse roles often search for BLS, ACLS, CCRN, PALS, NIHSS, TNCC, RN license, patients per shift, ventilated patients managed, ICU patient ratio, rapid response, and code blue experience.
