Chief Officer resume FAQ

What keywords should a Chief Officer put on a resume?

Chief Officer resumes should include deck operations, bridge operations, navigation, voyage planning, ECDIS, radar, cargo handling, ballast operations, ship stability, STCW, SOLAS, ISM Code, ISPS, MARPOL, safety management, emergency procedures, mooring operations, and crew supervision when they match your experience.

How do I make my Chief Officer CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact maritime keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on vessel types, cargo operations, navigation systems, safety compliance, deck leadership, bridge operations, and emergency responsibilities.

Why is my Chief Officer CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked on deck” do not show whether you managed cargo operations, planned voyages, used ECDIS, handled ballast operations, supervised deck crew, or ensured STCW, SOLAS, and ISM compliance.

Should I include vessel types and cargo operations on my Chief Officer CV?

Yes. Include vessel types such as tankers, bulk carriers, container ships, LNG carriers, offshore vessels, cruise ships, or supply vessels when relevant. Also include cargo operations, ballast operations, ship stability, cargo documentation, and loading or discharging experience.
