What actually happened

Before your resume reaches a recruiter, it goes through an ATS.

Resume → ATS Scan → Match Score → Recruiter

If your score is too low, your resume gets rejected automatically.

Check my ATS score →

Why your resume failed ATS

  • Missing keywords from the job description
  • Using different wording than the job post
  • Not tailored to the role
  • Low relevance compared to other candidates

Most resumes only match 30–55% of job requirements.

Find out your match score →

Quick check

  • Does your resume include the exact job title?
  • Are required skills written exactly as in the job post?
  • Does your experience clearly match the role?

If not, your resume is likely being filtered out.

Does formatting matter?

Yes — but less than most people think.

The biggest issue is still keyword mismatch.

Example

Job description: "CRM software experience"
Resume: "Used customer tools"

This difference alone can cause rejection.

How to fix your resume fast

  • Match your resume to each job
  • Use exact keywords from the job description
  • Focus on relevance, not volume
  • Keep formatting simple
