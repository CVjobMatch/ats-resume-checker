How resume match score works

Resume → Job Description → Keyword Match → Match Score → Shortlisted or Ignored

A resume match score is usually based on how closely your CV aligns with a job description. Important signals include job titles, skills, tools, responsibilities, keywords, and relevant proof from your experience.

Step-by-step: improve your match score

Step 1: Match your resume to the job description

Start with the real job description. Look for repeated skills, tools, responsibilities, certifications, and role-specific wording.

Step 2: Fix missing keywords naturally

Add important keywords only where they honestly match your background. Use the job description wording in your summary, skills, and experience sections.

Use a resume keyword optimizer to find missing terms faster.

Step 3: Focus on relevant experience

Move the most relevant experience higher on the page. Reduce details that do not support the target role.

Step 4: Show measurable results

Use numbers, outcomes, tools, volume, speed, savings, accuracy, revenue, or quality improvements where possible.

Step 5: Keep formatting clean

Use a simple resume format with clear sections. Avoid heavy graphics, tables, icons, and confusing layouts.

Job description: "Data analysis, SQL, dashboard reporting"
Resume: "Worked with data"

Worked with data • Helped reports • Assisted team

This may describe real work, but it does not clearly match the job description.

Better version

Analyzed data using SQL, built dashboard reports, and shared insights with the team.

The stronger version uses clearer wording, stronger keywords, and more specific role signals.

What usually lowers a resume match score

  • Missing keywords from the job description
  • Generic wording that could apply to any job
  • Important tools, systems, or certifications not listed
  • Relevant experience hidden too low on the resume
  • No measurable results or proof of impact
  • Using the same resume for every application

What usually improves a resume match score

  • Matching your resume to the job description
  • Using relevant resume keywords
  • Improving your skills section
  • Writing a stronger resume summary
  • Using a clean resume format
  • Showing results and responsibilities that match the role
