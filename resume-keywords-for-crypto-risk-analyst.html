Crypto Risk Analyst resumes should include market risk, liquidity risk, portfolio risk, credit risk, counterparty risk, VaR, stress testing, scenario analysis, volatility modeling, exposure analysis, DeFi risk, smart contract risk, token risk, Python, SQL, Excel, risk frameworks, and regulatory compliance when they match your experience.

Should I include VaR, stress testing, or Python on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. Crypto Risk Analyst roles often search for VaR, stress testing, scenario analysis, volatility modeling, Python, SQL, Excel, dashboards, and portfolio risk reporting.
