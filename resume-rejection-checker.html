Why resumes often get rejected

  • Important keywords from the job description are missing
  • The resume uses generic wording instead of role-specific language
  • The strongest experience is hidden too low on the page
  • The same resume is sent to every job
  • The CV does not clearly show why you should be shortlisted

Even a strong candidate can look like a weak match if the resume does not reflect the job description clearly.

Example of a weak match

Job description: "Data analysis, dashboard reporting, stakeholder communication"
Resume: "Worked with data and helped with reports"

Worked with data • Assisted reporting • Helped team

This wording is too vague and does not create a strong keyword match.

Better version

Analyzed weekly performance data • Built dashboard reports • Communicated insights to stakeholders

The improved version is clearer, more specific, and closer to the language employers often use in job descriptions.

What to check before applying again

  • Does your resume include the exact job title or a close variation?
  • Does your skills section match the job description wording?
  • Do your bullet points show relevant achievements?
  • Are required tools, systems, or certifications visible?
  • Would a recruiter understand your fit in under 10 seconds?
