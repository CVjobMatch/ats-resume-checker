Yes, if they match your experience. Fraud Analyst roles often search for tools such as Actimize, SAS Fraud Management, FICO Falcon, Sift, Riskified, SQL, Excel, and metrics such as fraud losses reduced, alerts reviewed, cases closed, false positives reduced, and chargebacks analyzed.
