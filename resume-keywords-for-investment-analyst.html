Investment Analyst resume FAQ

What keywords should an Investment Analyst put on a resume?

Investment Analyst resumes should include investment analysis, financial modeling, valuation, DCF, comparable company analysis, precedent transactions, equity research, market research, portfolio analysis, risk analysis, financial statements, capital markets, investment memos, due diligence, Excel, Bloomberg Terminal, FactSet, PitchBook, and corporate finance when they match your experience.

How do I make my Investment Analyst resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact Investment Analyst keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on modeling methods, valuation techniques, research duties, market analysis, finance tools, asset classes, investment memos, and measurable investment impact.

Why is my Investment Analyst resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in finance” do not show whether you built DCF models, performed valuation, conducted equity research, prepared investment memos, used Bloomberg, analyzed portfolios, or supported investment decisions.

Should I include tools and investment results on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. Investment Analyst roles often search for tools such as Excel, Bloomberg Terminal, FactSet, PitchBook, Capital IQ, Refinitiv Eikon, and outcomes such as models built, companies screened, memos prepared, deals evaluated, and portfolio performance tracked.
