Master Mariner resume FAQ

What keywords should a Master Mariner put on a CV?

Master Mariner and Captain CVs should include vessel command, ship captain, bridge management, voyage planning, navigation, maritime safety, STCW, SOLAS, ISM Code, Safety Management System, crew leadership, port operations, cargo operations, emergency response, incident command, SIRE vetting, audits, regulatory compliance, and operational decision-making when they match your experience.

How do I make my Master Mariner CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact captain-level keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on vessel command, vessel type, navigation, compliance, crew leadership, safety management, port operations, cargo operations, audit exposure, emergency response, and measurable vessel performance outcomes.

Why is my Captain CV not getting selected?

Your CV may still read like a Chief Officer profile. Phrases like “worked onboard vessel” do not show whether you commanded the vessel, led bridge operations, held final decision-making responsibility, managed compliance, prepared for vetting, or led emergency response.

Should I include audits, vetting, and safety performance on my Captain CV?

Yes, if they match your experience. Captain and Master Mariner roles often search for SIRE vetting, CDI inspections, PSC, flag state, class inspections, audit leadership, safety records, incident command, emergency response, ISM Code, SMS, and deficiency closure.
