Steward resume FAQ

What keywords should a Steward put on a CV?

Steward CVs should include guest service, hospitality, customer experience, housekeeping, cabin service, cleaning standards, laundry service, food and beverage service, table service, silver service, cruise ship operations, yacht service, offshore catering, STCW basic safety, hygiene standards, and safety procedures when they match your experience.

How do I make my Steward CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact steward keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on guest service, housekeeping, cabin service, laundry, food and beverage service, cruise, yacht, offshore, hotel operations, safety procedures, and service standards.

Why is my Steward CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “helped guests” or “cleaned rooms” do not show whether you handled cabin service, food and beverage service, housekeeping standards, laundry operations, guest satisfaction, safety procedures, or onboard hospitality.

Should I include STCW on my Steward CV?

Yes, if you have it. Steward roles on cruise ships, yachts, and offshore vessels may look for STCW basic safety, emergency response, hygiene, and safety procedure experience.
