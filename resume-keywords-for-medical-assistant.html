Medical Assistant resume FAQ

What keywords should a Medical Assistant put on a resume?

Medical Assistant resumes should include patient care, clinical support, vital signs, patient intake, EHR, EMR, Epic, Cerner, phlebotomy, injections, EKG, specimen collection, wound care, medication administration, infection control, patient preparation, room turnover, HIPAA compliance, appointment scheduling, medical records, and documentation when they match your experience.

How do I make my Medical Assistant resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact Medical Assistant keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on clinical procedures, patient care duties, EHR systems, administrative tasks, HIPAA, patient flow, clinic setting, and measurable workload.

Why is my Medical Assistant resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic or too administrative. Phrases like “worked in clinic” do not show whether you recorded vital signs, handled patient intake, used EHR systems, assisted with injections, performed EKGs, collected specimens, or supported patient flow.

Should I include EHR systems and patient volume on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. Medical Assistant roles often search for EHR, EMR, Epic, Cerner, Athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, patients per day, patient flow, provider support, room turnover, and clinical documentation.
