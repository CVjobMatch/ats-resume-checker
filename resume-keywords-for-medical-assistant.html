High-impact clinical keywords (often missing)

Phlebotomy • Injections • EKG • Specimen collection • Wound care • Medication administration • Infection control • Clinical procedures • Patient preparation • Room turnover

Administrative + system keywords

Insurance verification • Billing support • Coding basics • Patient scheduling • Front desk coordination • Medical documentation • EMR systems (Epic, Cerner)

What employers actually look for

  • Ability to support both clinical and admin tasks
  • Experience with patient intake and vital signs
  • Knowledge of EHR systems
  • Ability to work in fast-paced clinical environments
  • Strong patient communication skills

What most medical assistant resumes miss

  • No clinical procedures listed
  • No patient volume or workload
  • No EHR systems mentioned
  • Too much focus on admin tasks only

This is why many resumes don’t pass ATS.

How to improve your resume

  • Balance clinical and administrative keywords
  • Add procedures (vitals, injections, EKG)
  • Include EHR systems (Epic, Cerner)
  • Show patient volume and clinic pace
  • Match your resume to each job
