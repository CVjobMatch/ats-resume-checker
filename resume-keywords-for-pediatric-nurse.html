Pediatric Nurse resume FAQ

What keywords should a Pediatric Nurse put on a resume?

Pediatric Nurse resumes should include pediatric care, child healthcare, pediatric assessment, pediatric vital signs, weight-based dosing, medication administration, immunizations, growth monitoring, developmental milestones, family education, parent communication, NICU, PICU, pediatric ward, patient safety, and clinical documentation when they match your experience.

How do I make my Pediatric Nurse resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact pediatric nursing keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on pediatric care, child-specific monitoring, medication dosing, unit experience, family communication, certifications, EHR systems, and clinical documentation.

Why is my Pediatric Nurse resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in hospital” do not show whether you handled pediatric vital signs, administered weight-based medication, cared for children or neonates, worked in NICU or PICU, educated parents, or documented pediatric care.

Should I include NICU, PICU, and pediatric certifications on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. Pediatric Nurse roles often search for NICU, PICU, pediatric ward, neonatal care, pediatric emergency care, RN license, BLS, PALS, ACLS, NRP, immunizations, and pediatric specialty experience.
