First Engineer resume FAQ

What keywords should a First Engineer put on a CV?

First Engineer CVs should include engine room operations, marine diesel engines, main engines, auxiliary engines, pumps, compressors, boilers, fuel systems, HVAC, electrical systems, PMS, AMOS, preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, dry dock, spare parts management, STCW, ISM Code, and engine room safety when they match your experience.

How do I make my First Engineer CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact maritime engineering keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on engine systems, PMS tools, machinery, maintenance duties, troubleshooting, dry dock, safety procedures, vessel types, and measurable technical outcomes.

Why is my First Engineer CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in engine room” do not show whether you maintained main engines, managed PMS in AMOS, troubleshot pumps and compressors, supported dry dock, reduced downtime, or followed STCW and ISM Code procedures.

Should I include engine types and PMS tools on my First Engineer CV?

Yes, if they match your experience. First Engineer roles often search for engine makers, machinery systems, PMS tools, AMOS, main engines, auxiliary engines, pumps, compressors, boilers, dry dock, spare parts management, and troubleshooting experience.
