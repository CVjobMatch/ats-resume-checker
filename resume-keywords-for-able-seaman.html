This wording is too vague. It does not clearly show deck duties, maritime safety knowledge, watchkeeping, vessel maintenance, or the specific onboard tasks recruiters are looking for.

Performed deck operations, mooring, anchoring, and line handling duties. Supported navigation watch and lookout duties while following STCW safety procedures during daily vessel operations and emergency drills.
