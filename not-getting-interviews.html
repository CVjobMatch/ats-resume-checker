What’s actually happening when you apply

Before a recruiter reads your resume, it goes through an ATS (Applicant Tracking System).

Resume → ATS Scan → Match Score → Recruiter

If your match score is too low, your resume is filtered out automatically.

Why you're not getting interviews

  • Your resume doesn’t match the job description
  • You’re missing important keywords
  • Your wording doesn’t match what ATS systems expect
  • You’re sending the same resume to every job

Most resumes only match 30–55% of job requirements.

Quick self-check

  • Does your resume include the exact job title?
  • Are skills written exactly as in the job post?
  • Does your experience clearly match the role?

If not, your resume is likely being filtered out.
