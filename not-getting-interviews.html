What may be happening when you apply

Before a recruiter decides whether to interview you, your resume is often screened against the job description. That screening may involve ATS software, recruiter keyword searches, or a fast manual review.

Resume → Job Description → Keyword Match → ATS Score → Interview or Rejection

If your resume does not show the right keywords, skills, tools, responsibilities, certifications, or measurable results, your application can look weaker than it really is.

Example of a weak resume match

Job description: "Project management, stakeholder communication, delivery tracking"
Resume says: "Managed tasks"

Managed tasks • Helped projects • Assisted team

This may describe real work, but it does not clearly match the language or expectations in the job description.

Better resume wording

Coordinated project tasks, tracked delivery progress, communicated updates to stakeholders, and improved on-time delivery by 22%.

The improved version makes the fit clearer and gives both recruiters and ATS systems stronger signals.

Not getting interviews FAQ

Why am I not getting interviews?

You may not be getting interviews because your resume does not clearly match the job description. Common reasons include missing keywords, generic wording, weak role alignment, hidden achievements, missing tools or certifications, and using the same resume for every job.

Can a good resume still get no interviews?

Yes. A resume can look professional and still fail to get interviews if it does not match the exact job description. Recruiters and ATS systems may miss relevant experience if the resume does not include the right skills, tools, job titles, certifications, responsibilities, and measurable results.

How do I fix my resume if I am not getting interviews?

Compare your resume to the job description, add missing keywords naturally, rewrite generic bullet points, include relevant tools and certifications, move your strongest experience higher, and show measurable achievements that match the role.

Does ATS affect whether I get interviews?

ATS systems can affect whether your resume is easy to search, filter, rank, or review. A resume with missing keywords or weak alignment with the job description may appear less relevant before a recruiter reviews it.
