Typical General Practitioner responsibilities

  • Assess, diagnose, and treat acute and chronic conditions in primary care or outpatient settings
  • Manage patient panels, daily patient volume, follow-up care, and continuity of care
  • Provide chronic disease management for diabetes, hypertension, asthma, COPD, and cardiovascular risk
  • Deliver preventive care, vaccination programs, screenings, health education, and lifestyle counseling
  • Use EHR or EMR systems to maintain clinical documentation, patient records, ICD-10 coding, and referrals
  • Coordinate specialist referrals, diagnostic tests, multidisciplinary care, and patient follow-up
  • Communicate with patients and families about diagnosis, treatment plans, medication, prevention, and long-term health

General Practitioner resume FAQ

What keywords should a General Practitioner put on a CV?

General Practitioner CVs should include GP, General Practitioner, primary care, family medicine, outpatient care, clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning, patient management, chronic disease management, diabetes care, hypertension, preventive care, vaccination programs, referral management, EHR, EMR, ICD-10 coding, and clinical documentation when they match your experience.

How do I make my General Practitioner CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact GP keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on primary care duties, patient volume, chronic disease management, preventive care, EHR systems, clinical documentation, referral management, and measurable patient outcomes.

Why is my General Practitioner CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in clinic” do not show whether you managed patient volume, diagnosed acute and chronic conditions, handled diabetes or hypertension care, used EHR systems, coordinated referrals, or delivered preventive care.

Should I include patient volume and EHR systems on my GP CV?

Yes, if they match your experience. General Practitioner roles often search for patient volume, patients per day, primary care workload, EHR, EMR, Epic, Cerner, ICD-10 coding, clinical documentation, referral management, and patient records.
