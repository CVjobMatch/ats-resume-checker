What may be happening

Every time you apply, your resume may be screened by ATS software, recruiter keyword searches, or a quick manual review before anyone decides whether to interview you.

Resume → Job Description → Match Score → Shortlisted or Ignored

If your resume does not clearly show the right skills, keywords, tools, responsibilities, and results, your application can look weaker than it really is.

Learn more: how ATS resume checkers work.

Why you're getting no responses

  • Your resume does not clearly match job descriptions
  • You are missing key resume keywords
  • Your wording is too generic for the role
  • You are sending the same resume to every job
  • Your strongest experience is not visible quickly enough
  • Your CV does not make it obvious why you should be shortlisted

Applying to more jobs may not help if the same resume mismatch keeps happening.

Example of a weak match

Job description: "Data analysis, SQL, dashboard reporting"
Resume: "Worked with data"

Worked with data • Helped with reports • Assisted team

This may be true, but it does not clearly match the job description keywords or responsibilities.

Better version

Analyzed data using SQL, built dashboard reports, and shared insights with the team.

The stronger version is clearer, more specific, and easier to connect with the role.
