Customer Success Manager resume FAQ

What keywords should a Customer Success Manager put on a resume?

Customer Success Manager resumes should include SaaS, customer onboarding, customer retention, churn reduction, account management, renewals, expansion revenue, upselling, cross-selling, QBRs, customer health score, CRM, Salesforce, HubSpot, Gainsight, Totango, and stakeholder management when they match your experience.

How do I make my Customer Success Manager resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact customer success keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on SaaS, onboarding, retention, renewals, churn reduction, QBRs, expansion revenue, CRM tools, customer health, and measurable customer outcomes.

Why is my Customer Success Manager resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked with customers” do not show whether you managed SaaS accounts, reduced churn, led onboarding, handled renewals, ran QBRs, tracked customer health, or increased expansion revenue.

Should I include churn reduction and revenue metrics on my resume?

Yes, if you have them. Customer Success Manager roles often search for retention, churn reduction, renewals, expansion revenue, upselling, NRR, GRR, adoption growth, customer health, and measurable revenue or retention impact.
