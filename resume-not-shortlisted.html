What’s actually happening

Every time you apply, your resume is compared directly to the job description.

Resume → Match Score → Cutoff → Shortlist (or rejection)

If your score is too low, you are automatically rejected.

Why your resume is not getting shortlisted

  • You’re missing key job-specific keywords
  • Your wording doesn’t match the job description
  • Your experience is not clearly aligned
  • You’re sending the same CV to every job

Most resumes only match 30–55% of job requirements.

This is why you get no response.
