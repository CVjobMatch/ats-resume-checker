What may be happening

Every time you apply, your resume may be compared with the job description by ATS software, recruiter keyword searches, or a fast manual review.

Resume → Job Description → Match Score → Shortlist or Rejection

If your resume does not show the right skills, keywords, tools, responsibilities, and results, your application can look weaker than it really is.

Why your resume is not getting shortlisted

  • You are missing key job-specific keywords
  • Your wording does not match the job description closely enough
  • Your relevant experience is not clearly aligned with the role
  • Your strongest skills are hidden too low on the page
  • You are sending the same CV to every job

A resume can be strong in general but still fail to look relevant for one specific job.

Example of a weak shortlist match

Job description: "Project management, stakeholder communication, delivery tracking"
Your CV says: "Managed tasks and helped projects"

Managed tasks • Helped projects • Assisted team

This may describe real work, but it does not clearly match the job description or show enough role-specific value.

Better example

Coordinated project tasks, tracked delivery progress, and communicated updates to stakeholders.

The stronger version is clearer, more specific, and easier for recruiters and ATS systems to connect with the role.

Quick reality check

  • Does your CV include the exact job title or a close variation?
  • Do your skills match the wording in the job description?
  • Are required tools, systems, or certifications easy to find?
  • Would a recruiter understand the match in under 10 seconds?
  • Does your CV show results or responsibilities that fit the role?

If not, your resume may need stronger job-specific wording before you apply again.
