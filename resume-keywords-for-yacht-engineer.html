Yacht Engineer resume FAQ

What keywords should a Yacht Engineer put on a CV?

Yacht Engineer CVs should include superyacht engineering, engine maintenance, marine diesel engines, vessel systems, mechanical systems, electrical systems, HVAC, generators, hydraulics, PMS, troubleshooting, preventive maintenance, safety procedures, onboard operations, STCW, and ENG1 when they match your experience.

How do I make my Yacht Engineer CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the yacht engineer job description and add the exact yacht engineering keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on engines, vessel systems, PMS, troubleshooting, preventive maintenance, safety, certifications, shipyard work, and yacht experience.

Why is my Yacht Engineer CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked on boats” do not show whether you maintained engines, handled HVAC or electrical systems, used PMS, diagnosed faults, supported charter operations, completed refit work, or improved vessel reliability.

Should I include STCW and ENG1 on my Yacht Engineer CV?

Yes, if you have them. Yacht recruiters often look for STCW basic safety and ENG1 medical certification, especially for onboard yacht and superyacht engineering roles.
