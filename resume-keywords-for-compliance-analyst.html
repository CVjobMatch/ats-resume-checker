Compliance Analyst resume FAQ

What keywords should a Compliance Analyst put on a resume?

Compliance Analyst resumes should include regulatory compliance, risk assessment, internal controls, compliance testing, audit support, policy monitoring, issue tracking, remediation, governance, reporting, AML, KYC, SOX, GDPR, and relevant industry regulations when they match your experience.

How do I make my Compliance Analyst resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact compliance keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on regulations, frameworks, risk reviews, internal controls, audits, compliance testing, reporting, tools, and remediation work.

Why is my Compliance Analyst resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in compliance” do not show whether you performed risk assessments, tested controls, supported audits, monitored policies, prepared reports, tracked remediation, or worked with specific regulations.

Should I include compliance frameworks and tools on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. Include frameworks and regulations such as SOX, GDPR, FATF, Basel III, MiFID II, FCA, SEC, FINRA, HIPAA, PCI DSS, or ISO 27001, and tools such as Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, GRC systems, case management systems, or reporting tools when relevant.
