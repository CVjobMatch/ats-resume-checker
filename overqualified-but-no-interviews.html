What’s really happening

Before a recruiter sees your resume, it is filtered by an ATS.

Resume → Match Score → Relevance → Interview (or rejection)

If your resume doesn’t match the job closely, it gets filtered out — even if you're highly experienced.

👉 Learn more: how ATS works

Why you’re not getting interviews

  • Your experience looks unrelated to the job
  • Your resume includes too much irrelevant detail
  • You’re missing key keywords
  • Your job titles don’t match the role

To ATS systems, relevance matters more than experience level.

Example

Job: "Marketing Specialist"
Resume: "Senior Marketing Director"

This can confuse the system and lower your match score.

What actually works

  • Match your resume to the job
  • Focus on relevant experience only
  • Use exact keywords
  • Adjust your summary
