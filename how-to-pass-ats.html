How ATS works

Resume → ATS Scan → Match Score → Recruiter

If your score is too low, your resume is filtered out automatically.

Step-by-step: how to pass ATS

Step 1: Match your resume to the job description

Use the same keywords and wording as the job posting.

Step 2: Use exact keywords

ATS systems look for exact matches, not similar phrases.

Step 3: Fix your formatting

Avoid columns, tables, and graphics.

Step 4: Focus on relevant experience

Remove content that doesn’t match the role.

Step 5: Improve your match score

The higher your score, the better your chances.
