Third Engineer CVs should include engine room operations, marine diesel engines, auxiliary machinery, pumps, compressors, fuel systems, boiler systems, engine watchkeeping, PMS, preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, machinery systems, STCW, ISM Code, safety compliance, and vessel maintenance when they match your experience.

Why is my Third Engineer CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked as engineer onboard” do not show whether you maintained auxiliary machinery, handled engine watchkeeping, used PMS, operated pumps and compressors, troubleshot faults, or followed STCW and ISM safety procedures.

Should I include vessel types on my Third Engineer CV?

Yes. Include vessel types such as cargo ships, tankers, offshore vessels, bulk carriers, container ships, LNG carriers, chemical tankers, product tankers, supply vessels, or cruise ships when they match your experience.
