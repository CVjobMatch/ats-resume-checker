Crypto Compliance Analyst resume FAQ

What keywords should a Crypto Compliance Analyst put on a resume?

Crypto Compliance Analyst resumes should include AML, KYC, blockchain analysis, transaction monitoring, wallet screening, sanctions screening, SAR reporting, suspicious activity, FATF Travel Rule, OFAC sanctions, Chainalysis, TRM Labs, Elliptic, crypto exchanges, fintech compliance, and financial crime compliance when they match your experience.

How do I make my Crypto Compliance Analyst resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact crypto compliance keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on AML, KYC, wallet monitoring, blockchain transaction review, sanctions screening, tools, regulatory reporting, case investigations, and escalation work.

Why is my Crypto Compliance Analyst resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in finance” do not show whether you performed AML reviews, KYC checks, blockchain monitoring, wallet screening, sanctions reviews, SAR support, or crypto compliance investigations.

Should I include Chainalysis, TRM Labs, or Elliptic on my resume?

Yes, if you have used them. Crypto compliance roles often search for blockchain analytics tools such as Chainalysis, TRM Labs, Elliptic, CipherTrace, ComplyAdvantage, blockchain explorers, and case management systems.
