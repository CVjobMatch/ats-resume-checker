What does it mean to match your resume to a job description?

Matching your resume means aligning it directly with the job posting so ATS systems and recruiters can quickly see that your skills, experience, and wording fit the role.

  • Use important keywords from the job description
  • Highlight relevant skills and experience
  • Match job titles, responsibilities, tools, and requirements
  • Show clear alignment with what the employer is asking for

How resume matching works

Resume → Keyword Match → Match Score → Interview or Rejection

If your resume match score is too low, your application can look weaker than it really is. This often happens when your experience is relevant, but the wording does not match the job description.

Why most resumes do not match

  • Missing keywords from the job description
  • Using different wording than the employer uses
  • Including experience that is not relevant to this role
  • Sending the same resume to every job
  • Not making the strongest skills obvious enough

Many resumes only match part of the job requirements, even when the candidate has relevant experience.

Example of a poor resume match

Job description: "Project management, stakeholder communication, delivery tracking"
Resume: "Managed tasks and helped the team"

The resume may describe real experience, but it does not clearly match the keywords, responsibilities, or language in the job description.

This kind of mismatch can reduce your chance of getting shortlisted.

Quick resume match check

  • Does your resume include the exact job title or a close variation?
  • Are your skills written in the same language as the job description?
  • Does your experience clearly match the role requirements?
  • Are your strongest achievements easy to find?
  • Would a recruiter understand the fit in under 10 seconds?

If not, your resume may need stronger job-specific wording before you apply.

How to improve your resume match score

  • Use relevant wording from the job description naturally
  • Highlight measurable achievements that support the role
  • Remove or reduce irrelevant information
  • Move the most relevant experience higher on the page
  • Check your match score before applying
