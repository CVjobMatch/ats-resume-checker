What does it mean to match your resume to a job description?

Matching your resume means aligning it directly with the job posting so ATS systems and recruiters see you as a strong fit.

  • Use exact keywords from the job description
  • Highlight relevant skills and experience
  • Match job titles and responsibilities
  • Show clear alignment with the role

How resume matching works

Resume → Keyword Match → Match Score → Interview (or rejection)

If your match score is too low, your resume gets filtered out automatically.

Why most resumes don’t match

  • Missing keywords from the job description
  • Using different wording
  • Including irrelevant experience
  • Sending the same resume everywhere

Most resumes only match 30–55% of job requirements.

Quick match check

  • Does your resume include the exact job title?
  • Are your skills written exactly like the job description?
  • Does your experience clearly match the role?
  • Would a recruiter instantly see the fit?

If not, your match score is too low.

How to improve your match score

  • Use exact wording from the job description
  • Highlight measurable achievements
  • Remove irrelevant content
  • Check your match score before applying
