What is an ATS resume checker?

An ATS resume checker compares your resume or CV directly with a job description and calculates how closely they match.

The higher your score, the more likely you are to get interviews.

How ATS screening actually works

Resume / CV → ATS Scan → Match Score → Shortlist or Rejection

If your resume doesn’t match closely enough, it gets filtered out automatically.
