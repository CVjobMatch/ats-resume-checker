Typical Lab Technician responsibilities

  • Prepare, label, handle, and analyze laboratory samples or clinical specimens
  • Perform laboratory testing according to SOPs, GLP standards, and safety protocols
  • Operate, clean, maintain, troubleshoot, and calibrate laboratory equipment
  • Complete quality control checks, verify results, maintain accuracy, and support audit readiness
  • Record results in LIMS or laboratory documentation systems with strong data integrity
  • Handle reagents, chemicals, biological materials, PPE, waste disposal, and contamination prevention procedures
  • Communicate results, abnormal findings, equipment issues, and quality concerns to supervisors or lab teams
