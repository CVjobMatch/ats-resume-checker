Physiotherapist resume FAQ

What keywords should a Physiotherapist put on a CV?

Physiotherapist CVs should include physiotherapist, physical therapy, rehabilitation, patient assessment, treatment planning, mobility improvement, exercise therapy, manual therapy, musculoskeletal therapy, injury recovery, pain management, gait training, patient care, clinical documentation, and patient progress monitoring when they match your experience.

How do I make my Physiotherapist CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact physiotherapy keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on rehabilitation methods, therapy techniques, patient assessment, treatment planning, patient outcomes, clinical settings, documentation, and care team collaboration.

Why is my Physiotherapist CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked with patients” do not show whether you assessed mobility, created treatment plans, delivered exercise therapy, used manual therapy, monitored recovery, or documented clinical progress.

Should I include patient outcomes on my Physiotherapist CV?

Yes. Physiotherapist roles often value measurable patient outcomes such as improved mobility, reduced pain, better range of motion, improved balance, faster recovery, improved functional independence, or higher patient satisfaction.
