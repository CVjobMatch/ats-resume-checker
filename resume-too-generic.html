Quick self-check

Your resume is likely generic if:

  • You use the same resume for every job
  • Your skills section looks the same everywhere
  • You don’t use exact keywords from job descriptions
  • Your experience sounds general, not specific

If this sounds like you, your match score is probably low.

Why generic resumes fail

Generic Resume → Low Match Score → Filtered Out → No Interviews

Recruiters and ATS systems are not looking for general experience.

They are looking for exact matches.

Example

Generic resume:

Hardworking • Team player • Responsible • Good communication

Job-matching resume:

Customer support • CRM (Salesforce) • Ticket handling • Customer satisfaction metrics
