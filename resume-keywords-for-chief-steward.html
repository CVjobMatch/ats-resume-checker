Chief Steward resume FAQ

What keywords should a Chief Steward put on a resume?

Chief Steward resumes should include hospitality management, guest service, housekeeping management, cabin service, food and beverage operations, crew supervision, staff training, inventory control, scheduling, service standards, yacht service, cruise ship service, and STCW basic safety when they match your experience.

How do I make my Chief Steward CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact hospitality keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on guest service, housekeeping, food and beverage operations, inventory, crew supervision, staff training, vessel type, and onboard service standards.

Why is my Chief Steward CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked as steward” do not show whether you managed hospitality operations, supervised housekeeping, led a steward team, controlled inventory, trained staff, or delivered VIP guest service.

Should I include cruise ship or yacht experience on my Chief Steward CV?

Yes. Include cruise ship, luxury yacht, superyacht, offshore vessel, or private charter experience when relevant. Employers often search for vessel-specific experience, especially for roles involving guest service, housekeeping, food and beverage operations, and onboard crew leadership.
