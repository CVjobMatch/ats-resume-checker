Example of a weak Chief Engineer CV match

Job description: "Engine room management, marine diesel engines, PMS, ISM compliance, troubleshooting, dry dock"
CV says: "Worked onboard vessel"

Worked onboard vessel • Helped with maintenance • Assisted crew • Checked machinery

This wording is too vague. It does not clearly show engine room leadership, marine diesel engine responsibility, planned maintenance, troubleshooting, compliance, inspections, dry dock experience, or the specific maritime tasks recruiters are looking for.

Better Chief Engineer CV wording

Managed engine room operations, supervised engineering crew, maintained marine diesel engines and auxiliary machinery, used PMS for planned maintenance, led troubleshooting and inspections, and ensured STCW, ISM Code, SOLAS, and safety compliance.

Chief Engineer resume FAQ

What keywords should a Chief Engineer put on a resume?

Chief Engineer resumes should include engine room management, marine diesel engines, PMS, planned maintenance, troubleshooting, dry dock, ISM Code, STCW, SOLAS, MARPOL, crew management, technical inspections, vessel maintenance, machinery operation, and maritime compliance when they match your experience.

How do I make my Chief Engineer CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact maritime keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on vessel types, engine systems, maintenance responsibilities, compliance terms, certifications, inspections, dry dock work, and engineering leadership.

Why is my Chief Engineer CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked onboard vessel” do not show whether you managed the engine room, maintained marine diesel engines, used PMS, led troubleshooting, handled dry dock, managed crew, or ensured ISM and STCW compliance.

Should I include vessel types and engine systems on my Chief Engineer CV?

Yes. Include vessel types such as tankers, offshore vessels, LNG carriers, bulk carriers, container ships, or cruise ships when relevant. Also include systems such as main engines, auxiliary engines, generators, pumps, boilers, fuel systems, cooling systems, hydraulic systems, and power generation.
