Typical KYC Analyst responsibilities

  • Review customer files and perform KYC checks for onboarding, refresh, or periodic reviews
  • Conduct CDD, EDD, identity verification, document review, and customer risk assessment
  • Screen customers for sanctions, PEP status, adverse media, high-risk jurisdictions, and negative news
  • Review beneficial ownership, source of funds, source of wealth, business activity, and ownership structures
  • Maintain accurate KYC documentation, case notes, compliance records, and audit-ready files
  • Escalate red flags, high-risk customers, incomplete documentation, or suspicious activity to compliance teams
  • Use KYC, AML, screening, case management, and documentation tools to support banking, fintech, or financial services compliance

KYC Analyst resume FAQ

What keywords should a KYC Analyst put on a resume?

KYC Analyst resumes should include KYC, Know Your Customer, Customer Due Diligence, CDD, Enhanced Due Diligence, EDD, client onboarding, identity verification, customer risk profiling, AML compliance, sanctions screening, PEP screening, adverse media screening, beneficial ownership, source of funds, source of wealth, regulatory compliance, and financial crime prevention when they match your experience.

How do I make my KYC Analyst resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact KYC Analyst keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on CDD, EDD, client onboarding, identity verification, AML compliance, sanctions screening, PEP screening, documentation accuracy, case management, and measurable review outcomes.

Why is my KYC Analyst resume not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked with clients” do not show whether you performed CDD or EDD, verified identities, reviewed beneficial ownership, screened sanctions, handled PEP alerts, assessed customer risk, or maintained KYC documentation.

Should I include KYC tools and case metrics on my CV?

Yes, if they match your experience. KYC Analyst roles often search for tools such as World-Check, LexisNexis, Dow Jones Risk & Compliance, Actimize, Excel, case management systems, and metrics such as files reviewed, cases completed, QA accuracy, backlog reduction, and review turnaround time.
