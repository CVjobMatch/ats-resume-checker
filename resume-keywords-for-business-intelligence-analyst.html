BI Analyst job descriptions often mention reporting tools, SQL, dashboards, data models, KPIs, ETL, and stakeholder reporting. If your resume says only “worked with reports,” it may not show enough relevant detail.

What ATS and recruiters may look for

  • SQL and data querying experience
  • Power BI, Tableau, Looker, Qlik, or other BI tools
  • Dashboard development and KPI reporting
  • ETL, data pipelines, data warehouses, or data models
  • Stakeholder reporting, business insights, and decision support
  • Examples of reports, dashboards, or analytics work that created business value

Example of a weak BI Analyst resume match

Job description: "SQL, Power BI, dashboard development, KPI reporting"
Resume says: "Worked with reports"

Worked with data • Helped reporting • Assisted analysis

This wording is too vague. It does not clearly show BI tools, reporting responsibilities, or business impact.

Better BI Analyst resume wording

Built KPI dashboards in Power BI, queried data using SQL, automated recurring reports, and delivered business insights to stakeholders.

The stronger version gives clearer BI Analyst keywords and shows a better match with the job description.

What many BI Analyst resumes miss

  • No Power BI, Tableau, SQL, or dashboard language
  • No KPI reporting or stakeholder reporting
  • No ETL, data warehouse, or data modeling keywords when relevant
  • No measurable business impact from reports or dashboards
  • Too much generic language and not enough BI-specific wording
