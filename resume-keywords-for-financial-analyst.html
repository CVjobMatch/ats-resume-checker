Financial Analyst resume FAQ

What keywords should a Financial Analyst put on a resume?

Financial Analyst resumes should include financial modeling, forecasting, budgeting, variance analysis, financial reporting, KPI tracking, profitability analysis, DCF modeling, 3-statement modeling, valuation analysis, revenue forecasting, cost analysis, margin analysis, cash flow analysis, advanced Excel, Power BI, SQL, SAP, Oracle, and financial dashboards when they match your experience.

How do I make my Financial Analyst resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact financial analyst keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on modeling methods, reporting duties, finance tools, ERP systems, KPIs, forecasting, variance analysis, and measurable business impact.

Why is my Financial Analyst resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in finance” do not show whether you built models, created forecasts, performed variance analysis, used Excel or Power BI, analyzed margins, or improved financial reporting.

Should I include financial tools and measurable impact on my resume?

Yes. Financial Analyst roles often search for tools such as advanced Excel, Power BI, SQL, SAP, Oracle, Hyperion, Anaplan, Tableau, and measurable outcomes such as improved margins, reduced costs, faster reporting, better forecast accuracy, and profitability improvement.
