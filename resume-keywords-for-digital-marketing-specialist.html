How do I make my Digital Marketing Specialist resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact digital marketing keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on channels, tools, campaign types, analytics platforms, KPIs, performance marketing, optimization work, and measurable results.

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked on marketing” do not show whether you managed SEO, PPC, Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4 tracking, email campaigns, CRO tests, marketing dashboards, or measurable campaign performance.

Should I include marketing tools and metrics on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. Digital Marketing Specialist roles often search for tools such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Ahrefs, SEMrush, HubSpot, Mailchimp, CRM, and metrics such as ROAS, CTR, CPC, CPA, conversion rate, traffic growth, and lead generation.
