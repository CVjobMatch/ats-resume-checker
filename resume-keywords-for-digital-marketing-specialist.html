Example of weak resume

Job requires: "SEO and paid campaigns"
Resume says: "Worked on marketing"

Worked on marketing • Helped campaigns • Assisted team

Better example

Managed SEO strategy and keyword research • Ran Google Ads campaigns with 4.2 ROAS • Improved conversion rate by 25% • Tracked performance using Google Analytics (GA4)

What most marketing resumes miss

  • No tools listed (Google Ads, GA4, CRM)
  • No measurable results (traffic, conversions)
  • Too generic descriptions
  • No clear specialization (SEO vs PPC vs social)

This is why many resumes get rejected.

How to improve your resume

  • List specific tools and platforms
  • Show real results (CTR, ROAS, traffic)
  • Use exact keywords from job descriptions
  • Match your resume to each job
