Doctor resume FAQ

What keywords should a Doctor put on a CV?

Doctor CVs should include physician, doctor, patient care, clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment planning, medical procedures, clinical decision-making, evidence-based medicine, patient management, preventive care, clinical documentation, EHR, EMR, Epic, Cerner, ICD-10 coding, and multidisciplinary collaboration when they match your experience.

How do I make my Doctor CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact doctor keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on clinical skills, specialties, EMR systems, patient care, procedures, documentation, certifications, compliance, and measurable patient outcomes.

Why is my Doctor CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in hospital” do not show whether you assessed patients, diagnosed conditions, created treatment plans, used EMR systems, documented care, managed patient volume, or improved outcomes.

Should I include EMR systems and patient volume on my Doctor CV?

Yes, if they match your experience. Doctor roles often search for EMR and EHR systems such as Epic and Cerner, clinical documentation, ICD-10 coding, discharge summaries, and measurable patient volume such as patients managed per day.
