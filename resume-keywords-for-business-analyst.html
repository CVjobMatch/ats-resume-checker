Business Analyst job descriptions often mention requirements gathering, stakeholder management, business process analysis, documentation, user stories, Agile, UAT, reporting, and tools like Jira, Confluence, Excel, SQL, Power BI, or Tableau.

What ATS and recruiters may look for

  • Requirements gathering and documentation experience
  • Stakeholder communication and stakeholder management
  • Business process analysis and process improvement
  • Agile, Scrum, Jira, user stories, and acceptance criteria
  • UAT, test cases, validation, and business sign-off
  • SQL, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, KPI analysis, or reporting when relevant

Example of a weak Business Analyst resume match

Job description: "Requirements gathering, stakeholder management, Agile, Jira, UAT"
Resume says: "Worked with business teams"

Worked with teams • Helped projects • Assisted tasks

This wording is too vague. It does not clearly show business analysis responsibilities, tools, methods, or deliverables.

Better Business Analyst resume wording

Gathered business requirements, documented user stories in Jira, supported UAT, and coordinated stakeholder feedback to improve process delivery.

The stronger version gives clearer Business Analyst keywords and shows a better match with the job description.

What many Business Analyst resumes miss

  • No clear requirements gathering or stakeholder management language
  • No Jira, Confluence, Agile, Scrum, UAT, or user story keywords
  • No process improvement or business process analysis detail
  • No measurable impact from analysis, reporting, or workflow improvements
  • Too much generic language and not enough BA-specific wording
