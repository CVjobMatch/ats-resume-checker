Example of a weak Dentist resume match

Job description: "Dental procedures, fillings, extractions, treatment planning, dental X-rays, infection control"
Resume says: "Worked in clinic"

Worked in clinic • Assisted patients • Helped team • Performed dental work

This wording is too vague. It does not clearly show fillings, root canal treatment, tooth extraction, crowns, bridges, dental implants, oral diagnosis, dental X-rays, infection control, clinical documentation, or patient care responsibilities.

Better Dentist resume wording

Performed dental procedures including fillings, extractions, crowns, and root canal treatment, diagnosed oral conditions using dental X-rays and clinical examination, developed treatment plans, maintained patient records, and followed infection control and sterilization protocols.

The stronger version gives clearer Dentist keywords and shows a better match with the job description.

Typical Dentist responsibilities

  • Examine patients, diagnose oral conditions, and create treatment plans
  • Perform dental procedures such as fillings, extractions, crowns, bridges, and root canal treatment
  • Use dental X-rays, radiographs, digital imaging, and clinical examination to support diagnosis
  • Educate patients on oral hygiene, preventive care, treatment options, and follow-up needs
  • Maintain accurate patient records, clinical documentation, and treatment notes
  • Follow infection control, sterilization, patient safety, and dental compliance procedures
  • Coordinate with dental assistants, hygienists, specialists, and clinic staff

Dentist resume FAQ

What keywords should a Dentist put on a resume?

Dentist resumes should include dentist, DDS, DMD, dental procedures, restorative dentistry, preventive dentistry, fillings, root canal treatment, tooth extraction, crowns, bridges, dental implants, oral diagnosis, treatment planning, dental X-rays, radiographs, infection control, sterilization, patient care, and clinical documentation when they match your experience.

How do I make my Dentist resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact dentist keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on dental procedures, diagnosis, treatment planning, dental imaging, patient care, infection control, clinical documentation, licenses, certifications, and dental software.

Why is my Dentist resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in clinic” do not show whether you performed fillings, extractions, crowns, root canal treatment, dental X-rays, treatment planning, infection control, or patient counseling.

Should I include dental procedures and tools on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. Dentist roles often search for specific procedures and tools such as fillings, extractions, crowns, bridges, root canal treatment, dental implants, dental X-rays, radiographs, digital imaging, CBCT, dental software, and infection control.
