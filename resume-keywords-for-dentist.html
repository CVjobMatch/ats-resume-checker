What ATS looks for

  • Dental degree (DDS / DMD)
  • Experience with clinical procedures
  • Use of dental tools and imaging systems
  • Patient care and communication
  • Licensing and certifications
CV → Keyword Match → ATS Score → Interview

Example of weak CV

Job requires: "Dental procedures and patient care"
CV says: "Worked in clinic"

Worked in clinic • Assisted patients • Helped team

Better example

Performed dental procedures including fillings and extractions • Diagnosed oral conditions • Developed treatment plans • Maintained patient records • Ensured infection control compliance

What most dentist CVs miss

  • No specific procedures listed
  • No certifications or licensing mentioned
  • No tools or systems (X-ray, EMR)
  • Too generic descriptions

This is why qualified candidates still get rejected.

How to improve your dentist CV

  • List exact procedures you perform
  • Include certifications and licenses
  • Show tools and systems used
  • Highlight patient outcomes and results
  • Match your CV to each job
