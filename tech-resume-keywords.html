Why tech resumes get rejected

  • No match between resume and job description
  • Missing programming languages or tools
  • Too generic descriptions (no technical detail)
  • No measurable results or impact

How ATS evaluates your CV or resume

  • Exact keyword match (Python, Java, SQL)
  • Frameworks and tools (React, AWS, TensorFlow)
  • Job titles and role-specific phrases
  • Relevant experience aligned with the job posting

How to improve your tech resume

  • Match every resume to the job description
  • Use exact technical keywords from the job ad
  • Highlight real projects and systems built
  • Show measurable impact (performance, revenue, scale)
