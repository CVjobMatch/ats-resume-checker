Why tech resumes get rejected or ignored

  • The resume does not clearly match the job description
  • Important programming languages, tools, frameworks, or platforms are missing
  • Experience is described too generally without technical detail
  • Projects are listed without impact, scale, or business result
  • The same resume is sent to software, data, and AI roles without tailoring

Weak vs stronger tech resume wording

Job description: "Python, APIs, automation, data processing"

Worked on technical tasks and helped with data.
Built Python automation scripts, integrated REST APIs, and processed operational data to reduce manual reporting work.

The stronger version gives clearer technical signals and is easier to match against a real tech job description.

How ATS and recruiters evaluate tech resumes

  • Exact tools and languages, such as Python, SQL, React, AWS, or Docker
  • Role-specific phrases, such as backend development, data pipelines, or dashboard reporting
  • Relevant experience aligned with the job posting
  • Projects, systems, or products you actually built or improved
  • Measurable impact, such as performance, cost, revenue, speed, scale, or accuracy
