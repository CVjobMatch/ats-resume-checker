Crypto Analyst resume FAQ

What keywords should a Crypto Analyst put on a resume?

Crypto Analyst resumes should include blockchain analysis, cryptocurrency markets, on-chain analysis, tokenomics, DeFi, market research, financial modeling, risk analysis, portfolio analysis, trading strategies, Python, SQL, Dune Analytics, Nansen, Glassnode, Etherscan, and TradingView when they match your experience.

How do I make my Crypto Analyst resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact crypto keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on analysis methods, crypto tools, data skills, DeFi protocols, tokenomics, dashboards, market research, portfolio analysis, and risk work.

Why is my Crypto Analyst resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “interested in crypto” do not show whether you performed on-chain analysis, built dashboards, used SQL or Python, analyzed tokenomics, researched DeFi protocols, or prepared market reports.

Should I include dashboards or published crypto research on my resume?

Yes. Crypto Analyst roles often value proof of work. Include dashboards, research reports, GitHub projects, published analysis, investment memos, trading models, portfolio case studies, or Dune Analytics dashboards when they are relevant and safe to share.
