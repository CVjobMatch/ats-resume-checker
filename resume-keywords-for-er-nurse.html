ER Nurse resume FAQ

What keywords should an ER Nurse put on a resume?

ER Nurse resumes should include ER Nurse, Emergency Room Nurse, emergency care, triage, trauma care, patient stabilization, rapid response, acute care, critical care, emergency assessment, IV insertion, wound care, cardiac monitoring, medication administration, BLS, ACLS, PALS, Epic, Cerner, clinical documentation, and patient safety when they match your experience.

How do I make my ER Nurse resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact ER nursing keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on triage, trauma care, emergency procedures, patient stabilization, certifications, EMR systems, clinical documentation, patient safety, and measurable ER workload.

Why is my ER Nurse resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in hospital” do not show whether you performed triage, handled trauma care, stabilized patients, administered IV medications, used Epic or Cerner, followed emergency protocols, or worked in a high-volume ER.

Should I include certifications and ER patient volume on my resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. ER Nurse roles often search for BLS, ACLS, PALS, TNCC, ENPC, NIHSS, RN license, emergency department experience, high-volume ER, patients per shift, trauma care, and triage experience.
