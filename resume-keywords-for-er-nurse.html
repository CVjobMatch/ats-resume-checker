What most ER nurse resumes miss

  • No procedures listed (IV, monitoring, trauma)
  • No patient volume or ER intensity
  • No EMR system mentioned
  • No certifications beyond basic nursing

This is why experienced nurses still get rejected.

How to improve your ER nurse CV

  • List emergency procedures and trauma experience
  • Add certifications (BLS, ACLS, PALS)
  • Include EMR systems (Epic, Cerner)
  • Show real ER workload (patients per shift)
  • Match your resume to each job
