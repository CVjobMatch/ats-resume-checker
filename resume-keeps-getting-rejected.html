Why your resume keeps getting rejected

  • Your resume does not clearly match the job description
  • You are missing important resume keywords
  • Your wording is too generic for the role
  • You are sending the same resume to every job
  • Your strongest experience is not obvious enough

A strong candidate can still get rejected if the resume does not make the job match clear.

Quick check

  • Does your resume include the exact job title or a close variation?
  • Are your skills written in similar wording to the job post?
  • Does your experience clearly match the role requirements?
  • Are important tools, systems, or certifications visible?
  • Would a recruiter understand the fit in under 10 seconds?

If not, your resume may need stronger job-specific wording before you apply again.

Example of a weak match

Job description: "Data analysis, dashboard reporting, stakeholder communication"
Resume: "Worked with data"

Worked with data • Assisted reporting • Helped team

This may be true, but it does not clearly show the keywords and responsibilities from the job description.

Better version

Analyzed weekly performance data • Built dashboard reports • Communicated insights to stakeholders

The improved version makes the fit clearer for both recruiters and ATS systems.
