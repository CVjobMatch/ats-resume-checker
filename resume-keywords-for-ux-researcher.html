Top UX researcher resume keywords

User research • Qualitative research • Quantitative research • Usability testing • User interviews • Survey design • A/B testing • UX strategy • Research insights • Data analysis • Persona development • Journey mapping • Wireframing • Figma • User-centered design • Stakeholder communication

What ATS looks for in UX researcher resumes

  • Experience conducting user research (interviews, usability testing)
  • Strong analytical and research skills
  • Tools (Figma, UserTesting, Hotjar, Maze)
  • Ability to translate insights into product decisions
  • Collaboration with product, design, and engineering teams
