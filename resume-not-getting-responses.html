Why your resume is not getting responses

When you apply, your resume may be screened by ATS software, recruiter keyword searches, or a fast manual review. If the match is unclear, your application can be skipped even if you have relevant experience.

  • Missing keywords from the job description
  • Generic resume wording that is not tailored to the role
  • Different wording than the job posting
  • Weak or unclear resume match score
  • Relevant experience hidden too far down the page

What actually works

  • Match your resume to the job description
  • Use important keywords from the job post naturally
  • Highlight relevant experience clearly
  • Make your strongest skills visible quickly
  • Improve the way your resume explains your fit

How resume screening works

Resume → Job Description → Match Score → Shortlisted or Ignored

A resume can be good in general but still fail for one specific job if it does not reflect the job description closely enough.

That is why sending the same resume everywhere often leads to low response rates.

Example of a weak match

Job description: "Customer support, CRM, ticket resolution, follow-up"
Resume: "Worked with customers"

Worked with customers • Helped clients • Handled admin

Better version

Handled customer support tickets in CRM, followed up open cases, and resolved customer issues through clear communication.

The better version gives recruiters and ATS systems clearer signals that the experience matches the role.
