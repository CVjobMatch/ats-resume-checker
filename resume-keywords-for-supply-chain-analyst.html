Supply Chain Analyst resume FAQ

What keywords should a Supply Chain Analyst put on a resume?

Supply Chain Analyst resumes should include supply chain management, demand forecasting, demand planning, supply planning, S&OP, inventory management, inventory optimization, logistics, procurement, vendor management, ERP, SAP, Oracle, Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, KPI tracking, OTIF, fill rate, forecast accuracy, and cost reduction when they match your experience.

How do I make my Supply Chain Analyst resume ATS-friendly?

Compare your resume to the job description and add the exact supply chain keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on planning, forecasting, inventory, logistics, ERP systems, analytics tools, KPIs, cost optimization, and measurable operations outcomes.

Why is my Supply Chain Analyst resume not getting interviews?

Your resume may be too generic. Phrases like “worked in operations” do not show whether you handled demand forecasting, inventory optimization, SAP reporting, SQL analysis, Power BI dashboards, KPI tracking, or cost reduction projects.

Should I include KPIs and tools on my Supply Chain Analyst resume?

Yes, if they match your experience. Supply Chain Analyst roles often search for KPIs such as OTIF, fill rate, forecast accuracy, inventory turnover, service level, and cost reduction, plus tools such as SAP, Oracle, Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, MRP, WMS, and TMS.
