The best resume format (structure)

Name
Contact Information

Professional Summary

Work Experience
- Role
- Achievements (with results)

Skills

Education

Why this format works

  • Easy for ATS to read
  • Clear structure for recruiters
  • Focuses on content, not design

Formatting rules (important)

  • Use a single column layout
  • Avoid tables and graphics
  • Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri)
  • Use clear section headings

What matters more than format

Keyword match and relevance.
