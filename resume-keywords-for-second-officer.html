Second Officer resume FAQ

What keywords should a Second Officer put on a CV?

Second Officer CVs should include navigation, voyage planning, passage planning, ECDIS, bridge watchkeeping, chart corrections, radar, ARPA, GPS navigation, GMDSS, bridge operations, STCW, SOLAS, safety procedures, emergency response, maritime communication, and vessel operations when they match your experience.

How do I make my Second Officer CV ATS-friendly?

Compare your CV to the job description and add the exact maritime navigation keywords that honestly match your experience. Focus on bridge watchkeeping, voyage planning, ECDIS, radar, GPS, GMDSS, chart corrections, safety procedures, certifications, and vessel types.

Why is my Second Officer CV not getting interviews?

Your CV may be too generic. Phrases like “worked as officer” do not show whether you handled voyage planning, operated ECDIS and radar, stood bridge watch, completed chart corrections, maintained logbooks, or followed STCW and SOLAS requirements.

Should I include vessel types on my Second Officer CV?

Yes. Include vessel types such as cargo ships, tankers, offshore vessels, bulk carriers, container ships, LNG carriers, chemical tankers, product tankers, supply vessels, cruise ships, or passenger vessels when they match your experience.
